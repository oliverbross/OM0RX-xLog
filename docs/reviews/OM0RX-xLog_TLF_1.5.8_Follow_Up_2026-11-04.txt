3. R09 — LOST SUPPRESSION DECISION AT THE HOST/NATIVE BOUNDARY

Priority: Medium; complete this before accepting ordinary early-start ESM.

- Claim the finish operation and record accepted WinKeyer native-queue evidence.
- Supply the valid current native owner/provider/Host admission and execute callsign ESM.
- Do not put a synthetic suppression marker on a fabricated exchange.

The actual Host returns one action:
  key: F3
  template: {RST} {SENTNR}
  text: 5NN TT1
  earlyCallSuppressed: absent

Meanwhile earlyCW.esmConsumed is true. Reserved serial remains 1 and no QSO is logged.

Confirmed production cause:
- python/contest_host/host.py:765–771 sets earlyCallSuppressed=true on the HISCALL action and empties CALL after valid admission.
- host.py:779–782 drops that action because the default {CALL} macro is now empty.
- prototype/src-tauri/src/cw_session.rs:285–286 looks only for the marker in returned actions, so suppressed=false.
- cw_session.rs:328–334 takes the live continuation but selects ordinary sequence rather than continue_sequence.
- cw_session_backend.rs:204–206 invokes winkeyer_sequence without continuationStreamID; the correct continuation route at 216–218 supplies it.
- winkeyer.rs:231–239 and 248–267 reject ordinary send while the keyer still owns the queued/keying call. Finishing the stream does not itself release that ownership.

This can make a fast normal Enter flow fail to append its exchange and discard the live continuation. If the call has already drained, ordinary sending may work; do not describe every ESM send as broken.

The new native test misses the real contract: cw_session.rs:589–592 puts earlyCallSuppressed directly on a nonempty synthetic exchange. The live test at 703–716 uses that fabricated shape.

Required correction:
Carry the Host's fresh, operation-scoped suppression/continuation decision across the boundary even when its HISCALL text action becomes empty. Use that authoritative decision together with the existing live owner/epoch, provider identity/generation, Host epoch, draft/stream and one-use admission checks to choose the existing continuation route.

An operation-level decision or another explicit owner-issued contract can work; choose the smallest robust fit. Do not infer permission solely from persisted esmConsumed, merely from the presence of some old continuation, or from renderer-supplied data. Do not restore the old unsafe restart behavior.

Meaningful regression:
