# macOS 1.3.34 — retain requested switches until admitted work finishes

Accepted application source before this version/provenance commit:
`ba53ca7dea7bf665c55a4786cc0aeaa184efa7e0`.

Installed 1.3.33 deferred cosmetic spot removal correctly in isolated process
checks, but its first live Return to Normal was rejected before retirement by
the generic current-operating-action guard. Sixteen fresh contest-owned marker
receipts existed. Its exact overlapping admission was not exposed in the UI.
The app and data remained usable, and no repeat switch or restart was used to
claim acceptance.

The explicit switch now atomically records its intent, prevents new admissions
and waits for previously accepted work to finish. The native worker gate also
seals while retaining its in-flight count, then drains that count. Both waits
are bounded at 30 seconds. Pending data/control operations keep their identity
and protection; retirement and preference commit still require their completion.
A pre-retirement timeout restores admission without discarding pending work.
Cosmetic spot removal remains deferred by the 1.3.33 fix.

Validation: all 888 native cases passed, including held-operation completion,
new-admission exclusion, competing requests and timeout recovery. All 15
operating-module tests passed again after the final wake/context checks.
The earlier exact-worker fresh/expired marker cases passed against signed
1.3.33; this release retains those sources and will repeat its bundled-worker
checks. Analytics is preserved separately; CW remains hard-disabled.
