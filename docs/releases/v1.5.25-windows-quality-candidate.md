### Acknowledged Settings save and authoritative readback

Acceptance records the baseline generation/revision, waits for that exact submission, then performs one authoritative post-completion read and checks the expected generation, revision and station call. The form blocks duplicate submissions while awaiting completion. The initial focused attempt remains retained separately (eight failed, 19 passed and eight reported errors); the corrected second attempt is the passing receipt. These source checks do not establish new packaged or ordinary-desktop acceptance.
