Development diagnostics can further restrict the selected matrix with the closed comma-separated `XLOG_FAMILY_ACCEPTANCE_PROFILE_IDS` environment variable. Every identifier must belong to the chosen scenario; duplicate, empty, whitespace-padded and unknown identifiers fail before browser work. Omitting the filter requires exactly 16 legacy or 12 declarative cases. The runtime catalog is checked against the full harness identity/mode table. The inactive ARRL RTTY Roundup and NAQP RTTY Plain Log rules are explicit exclusions because they have no admitted simulated operator capability. This coverage is limited to active catalog operator profiles and does not claim semantic coverage of the 94-plugin source metadata inventory. The matrix is finite and identity-bound. It covers CQ WW, CQ WPX, Oceania DX, SAC, NAQP, JIDX and ARRL DX in CW and SSB. Each of the 16 legacy-compatible sessions logs three Run contacts on 20 metres and three Search & Pounce contacts on 40 metres. Captured bytes stay inside the private acceptance root.
